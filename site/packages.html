FAQ

About pricing.

Why does it say “Price on request”?

Every project is a little different, so we confirm a fixed price after a quick brief. Send your brief through the Project Planner or tap “Get a quote” and you will get a clear number before anything starts.

Can I change what is in a package?

Yes. Packages are starting points. Tell us what you need to add or remove and we will adjust the quote.

Can I combine several services into one project?

Yes. Pick everything you need in the Project Planner and Moiz will quote it as one project, with one plan and one point of contact.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the scope and how quickly content and feedback come in. Your quote includes a timeline, and you can tell us your preferred deadline in the Project Planner.

How do revisions work?

Every package includes revision rounds, listed on the package. We share work at clear checkpoints so feedback is easy, and extra rounds are available as an add-on.
