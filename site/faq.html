FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about working with Mogify Studio. Anything else, ask on WhatsApp.

Working with us

How much does a project cost?

It depends on what you need. Each package on the Packages page lists exactly what is included. Send your brief through the Project Planner and Moiz replies with a clear, fixed quote before any work starts.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the scope and how quickly content and feedback come in. Your quote includes a timeline, and you can tell us your preferred deadline in the Project Planner.

Do you work with clients outside Karachi and Pakistan?

Yes. Mogify Studio is based in Karachi and works with businesses across Pakistan and worldwide. Everything runs smoothly over WhatsApp, email and video calls.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short brief is enough: what you need, your goals and any references you like. The Project Planner walks you through it in about two minutes and sends it straight to us.

How do revisions work?

Every package includes revision rounds, listed on the package. We share work at clear checkpoints so feedback is easy, and extra rounds are available as an add-on.

Can I get a custom package?

Yes. If your project mixes services (for example a logo, website and launch reel), pick them all in the Project Planner and we will put together one package for you.

Who will I be working with?

You talk directly with Muhammad Moiz Ali, the founder of Mogify Studio, from the first message to the final files. No account managers in between.

Can you build my website in Urdu or both languages?

Yes, a bilingual English and Urdu version with proper right-to-left layout is available as an add-on.

Do you only work on big projects?

No. Packages start from a single logo, a one-page website or a short video. Tell us what you need and we will tell you honestly what fits.

About our services

Do I need to have my content ready?

It helps, but it is not required. We can structure your pages and help with the words; copywriting is also available as an add-on.

Will I get the source files?

Final files come in PNG, JPG, SVG and PDF. Editable source files are included in Growth and Premium, and available as an add-on with Starter.

What is the difference between a logo and a brand identity?

A logo is one mark. A brand identity is the full system around it: colours, type, layouts, templates and rules, so everything you publish looks consistent.

Do you also develop the app?

Our focus is design and front-end for the web. For native apps we deliver developer-ready designs your team or developer can build from.

Can you animate my existing logo?

Yes. Send the logo file (ideally SVG or AI) and we will design a reveal that fits your brand.

What do you need to model my product?

Clear photos from several angles and the real dimensions are enough to start. CAD or 3D files make it faster.

Do you post for us too?

Our focus is design. We deliver ready-to-post files and a calendar; posting can be discussed in your quote.

Do you set up files for Amazon KDP?

Yes. Growth and Premium include print-ready files set up to KDP or IngramSpark specs, including spine width.

How do I send my footage?

Share a Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer link; we send back the finished files the same way.
